Double-click a clip's header to rename it. A clip named after its content is
easier to find than one named Audio 1 once a project holds more than a few
clips.

A clip's context menu, on right-click or its **⋯** button, holds cut and
copy, duplicate, the split and delete variants, pitch and speed, and **Clip
color**.

  name="app/SceneClipMenu"

/>

<Callout type="tip" title="A second track is scratch space">
  A clip that has no home yet can be dragged to a spare track and parked
  there. Tracks cost nothing, everything on them stays in sync as you edit,
  and muting the scratch track keeps it silent until you decide.
</Callout>
