Audio in a project lives inside **clips**: blocks of sound sitting on
tracks. Recording creates a clip, importing a file creates a clip, and
nearly every edit acts on one.

- **[The clip](/manual/clips/the-clip)**: its header, menu, handles and
  waveform.
- **[Selecting and moving](/manual/clips/selecting-and-moving)**: picking
  clips and time ranges, and dragging clips between tracks.
- **[Trim and stretch](/manual/clips/trim-and-stretch)**: the two pairs of
  handles, and what each does.
- **[Split](/manual/clips/split)**: cutting one clip into two.
- **[Clip gain](/manual/clips/clip-gain)**: the volume curve drawn on the
  clip itself.

For a guided walk through the same ground, see
[Basic audio editing](/manual/getting-started/basic-audio-editing).
