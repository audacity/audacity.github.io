## Install FFmpeg

Audacity handles the common formats on its own, but patent restrictions mean
FFmpeg can't be bundled with it. Without FFmpeg installed, formats like M4A and
WMA won't import or export — which is usually the first wall people hit after
the basics.

[Installing FFmpeg](/manual/basics/installing-ffmpeg) walks through it on
Windows, macOS and Linux.
