The Project toolbar runs across the very top of the window. Nothing on it
touches your audio directly — it is where you move between views, reach the
audio and sharing dialogs, and shape the workspace itself.

<UIShot
  name="app/ToolbarProject"
  alt="The Project toolbar: tabs, hotbar, workspace selector, undo and redo"
/>

## Home and Project tabs

The tabs at the left switch the whole window between the
[Home screen](/manual/manual-index/project-management-menu) — your projects,
recent files and cloud library — and the **Project** view where editing
happens.

## The hotbar

The group of quick-access buttons in the centre. Each one opens a menu or a
dialog:

<UIShot
  name="toolbars/ProjectToolBar"
  alt="The centre toolbar group: Audio setup, Share audio and Get effects"
/>

- [Audio setup](/manual/manual-index/hotbar/audio-setup) — choose the audio
  host, the playback and recording devices, and the number of recording
  channels.
- [Share audio](/manual/manual-index/hotbar/share-audio) — upload a rendered
  copy of the project to audio.com.
- [Get effects](/manual/manual-index/hotbar/get-effects) — browse effect
  plugins on MuseHub.

## Workspace selector

The dropdown on the right switches between the
[workspaces](/manual/manual-index/workspaces) — Classic, Modern, Music and
Spectral editing — changing which tools the
[Tools toolbar](/manual/manual-index/toolbar) shows.

## Undo and redo

The two arrows at the far right step backwards and forwards through your
edits — the same [Undo and Redo](/manual/manual-index/header/edit) as the
Edit menu, kept in reach.

Share audio only appears in builds where cloud support is enabled.

## Where to find it

Along the top of the project window, between the Home and Project tabs on
the left and the workspace selector and Undo/Redo on the right. When the
window is too narrow for the labels, the buttons drop to icons only.

The buttons need a project to be open.
