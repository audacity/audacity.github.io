The Hotbar is the group of quick-access buttons in the centre of the bar
across the top of the project window. None of them change your audio
directly — each one opens a menu or a dialog.

## What it contains

<UIExample component="project-toolbar" variant="default" interactive client:load />

- [Audio setup](/manual/manual-index/hotbar/audio-setup) — choose the audio
  host, the playback and recording devices, and the number of recording
  channels.
- [Share audio](/manual/manual-index/hotbar/share-audio) — upload a rendered
  copy of the project to audio.com.
- [Get effects](/manual/manual-index/hotbar/get-effects) — browse effect
  plugins on MuseHub.

Share audio only appears in builds where cloud support is enabled.

## Where to find it

Along the top of the project window, between the Home and Project tabs on
the left and the workspace selector and Undo/Redo on the right. When the
window is too narrow for the labels, the buttons drop to icons only.

The buttons need a project to be open.
