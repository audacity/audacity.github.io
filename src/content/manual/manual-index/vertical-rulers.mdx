The vertical rulers are a narrow strip down the right-hand edge of the tracks
area. Each audio track gets its own ruler, lined up with that track's height,
giving you a scale to read the waveform against. Label tracks have no ruler.

They are hidden by default. Turn them on with
[View → Show vertical rulers](/manual/manual-index/header/view#show-vertical-rulers),
or by right-clicking the
[timeline ruler](/manual/manual-index/timeline/show-vertical-rulers). The
setting applies to the whole application, not to one project.

## What the rulers show

For a track shown as a waveform, the ruler is an amplitude scale. For a track
shown as a spectrogram, it is a frequency scale instead. A track showing both
views gets both, stacked to match, and a stereo track gets one scale per
channel.

## Changing a ruler

Click a waveform ruler to open its options:

- **Zoom in**, **Zoom out** and **Reset** — change the vertical zoom of that
  track's waveform, or return it to the default.
- **Ruler format** — choose between **Logarithmic (dB)**, **Linear (dB)** and
  **Linear (amp)**.
- **Half wave** — show only half of the waveform rather than both sides of
  the centre line.

The choices are made per track and saved with the project.
