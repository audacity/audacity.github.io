Turns looping on and off. While it is on, playback repeats the loop region —
the stretch marked out in the upper half of the timeline ruler — instead of
running on to the end of the project. Turning it off leaves the region
defined but inactive, so you can switch it back on later without setting it
up again.

Drawing a new loop region on the ruler turns looping on for you.

## Where to find it

Right-click the timeline ruler and choose **Toggle loop region**, or press
<Shortcut client:load keys="l" />. It is also in the
[Looping](/manual/manual-index/header/select/looping) submenu and on the
[toolbar](/manual/manual-index/toolbar/toggle-loop-region).
