Sets the timeline ruler to musical time — bars and beats rather than clock
time. Where the bar and beat lines fall is worked out from the project
[tempo](/manual/manual-index/toolbar/tempo) and
[time signature](/manual/manual-index/toolbar/time-signature), so changing
either changes the ruler. As you zoom in, the ruler subdivides the beat
further.

In this format the grid behind the tracks also picks up faint alternating
shading, so bars are easier to read at a glance.

This is one of two ruler formats; the other is
[Minutes & seconds](/manual/manual-index/timeline/minutes-and-seconds).
Choosing one turns the other off.

## Where to find it

Right-click the timeline ruler and choose **Beats & measures**. The item is
checked while it is the active format.
