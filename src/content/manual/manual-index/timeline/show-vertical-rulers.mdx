Shows or hides the vertical rulers — the strip down the right-hand edge of
the tracks area that gives each audio track its own amplitude scale. See
[Vertical Rulers](/manual/manual-index/vertical-rulers) for what the rulers
show and how to change their format.

They are hidden by default, and the setting applies to the whole application
rather than to one project.

## Where to find it

Right-click the timeline ruler and choose **Show vertical rulers**, or use
[View → Show vertical rulers](/manual/manual-index/header/view/show-vertical-rulers).
The item is checked while the rulers are shown.
