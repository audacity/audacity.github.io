Generates a drum sound of the kind described by Jean-Claude Risset: a set of
inharmonic partials over a low fundamental, mixed with a narrow band of
noise, all decaying together. The result sits somewhere between a bell and a
drum and is easy to tune, so it works well for percussion and for
sound-design material.

## Where to find it

The **Generate** menu.

## Controls

- **Frequency (Hz)** — 50 to 2000, default 100. The fundamental pitch of the
  drum.
- **Decay (seconds)** — 0.1 to 60, default 2. How long the sound takes to die
  away; this is also the length of the generated audio.
- **Center frequency of noise (Hz)** — 100 to 5000, default 500. Where the
  noise component sits.
- **Width of noise band (Hz)** — 10 to 1000, default 400. How broad that
  noise band is.
- **Amount of noise in mix (percent)** — 0 to 100, default 25. How much noise
  is blended with the tonal part.
- **Amplitude (0 - 1)** — 0 to 1, default 0.8. The peak level of the result.

## Notes

More noise and a wider band give a drier, more percussive attack; less noise
leaves the inharmonic tone exposed and the sound turns bell-like. A short
decay with the fundamental low gives a usable kick, while longer decays drift
towards a gong.
