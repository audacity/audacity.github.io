Effects in this group change the character of a sound rather than its level
or its frequency balance — by varying it cyclically over time, or by using
one signal to shape another.

## Where to find it

The **Distortion and modulation** submenu of the **Effect** menu, between
Delay and reverb and Special.

## What is in it

- [Tremolo](/manual/manual-index/egat/effects/distortion-and-modulation/tremolo)
  — vary the volume cyclically at a chosen rate and waveform.
- [Vocoder](/manual/manual-index/egat/effects/distortion-and-modulation/vocoder)
  — impose the changing spectrum of one signal onto a carrier.
