The Legacy group holds effects that have been replaced by newer versions but
are still shipped, so that projects and workflows built around them keep
working. Reach for the current equivalent first; use a legacy effect when you
need to match the behaviour of something you did before.

## Where to find it

The **Legacy** submenu of the **Effect** menu, after Spectral tools.

## What is in it

- [Legacy Limiter](/manual/manual-index/egat/effects/legacy/legacy-limiter) —
  the Nyquist limiter that preceded the built-in
  [Limiter](/manual/manual-index/egat/effects/volume-and-compression/limiter).
