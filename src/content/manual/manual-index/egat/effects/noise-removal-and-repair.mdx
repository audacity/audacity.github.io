These effects are for fixing problems rather than shaping sound: hiss or hum
running under a whole recording, clicks and crackle scattered through it, a
gate to shut the gaps between phrases, and a repair tool for very short
stretches that are damaged beyond filtering.

## Where to find it

The **Noise removal and repair** submenu of the **Effect** menu, with audio
selected.

## Notes

Clean-up belongs early, before levelling or equalisation, because a noise floor
that is left in place gets raised along with everything else.

All of these effects trade fidelity against the problem they are removing. Use
the smallest setting that solves it: pushing noise reduction hard produces a
distinctive bubbling or metallic quality that is more distracting than the
noise was.
