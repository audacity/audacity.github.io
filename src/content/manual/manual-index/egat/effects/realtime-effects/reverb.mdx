This is the same effect as
[Reverb](/manual/manual-index/egat/effects/delay-and-reverb/reverb) in the
Effect menu, added to a track's effect stack instead of applied to its audio.
The controls and what they do are identical — that page has the reference —
but nothing is written back to the track: the reverb is produced as the project
plays.

  name="effects/Reverb-realtime"

/>

## Where to find it

Click the **Effects** button across the bottom of the track's control panel, or
press **E**, then **Add effect** and choose **Reverb**.

## Working with it

Because the audio is untouched, everything stays adjustable. Change the room
size while the project is playing and you hear the result immediately; bypass
the effect with its power button to compare against the dry track; move it
above or below the other effects on the track to change where in the chain it
sits; take it off the stack and the track is exactly as it was.

The reverb is included when you export, so there is no step at which you have
to apply it.

## Notes

Reverb is the effect that benefits most from being realtime, because the right
amount depends on everything else in the mix. Set it while the whole project is
playing rather than by soloing the track.

Applied as a track effect there is no need to leave silence for the tail: the
reverb continues past the end of the audio on its own.

A common arrangement is one reverb on the **Master effects** section at the
bottom of the effects panel, giving every track the same room, rather than a
separate reverb per track.

See [Realtime Effects](/manual/manual-index/egat/effects/realtime-effects) for
the stack as a whole.
