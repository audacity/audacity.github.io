This is the same effect as
[Compressor](/manual/manual-index/egat/effects/volume-and-compression/compressor)
in the Effect menu, added to a track's effect stack instead of applied to its
audio. The controls and what they do are identical — that page has the
reference — but nothing is written back to the track: the compression happens
as the project plays.

## Where to find it

Click the **Effects** button across the bottom of the track's control panel, or
press **E**, then **Add effect** and choose **Compressor**.

## Working with it

Because the audio is untouched, everything stays adjustable. Change the
threshold while the project is playing and you hear the result immediately;
bypass the effect with its power button to compare against the original; move
it above or below the other effects on the track to change where in the chain
it sits; take it off the stack and the track is exactly as it was.

The compression is included when you export, so there is no step at which you
have to apply it.

## Notes

Used this way the Compressor shows a metering panel above the knobs, with
**Show** toggles for **Input**, **Output** and **Compression**, so you can
watch how much gain reduction is happening while the project plays. Applied
destructively from the Effect menu there is nothing to meter and the panel is
not shown.

Order matters. Compression before reverb tightens the source and then adds
space to the result; reverb before compression pumps the reverb tail along with
the signal.

See [Realtime Effects](/manual/manual-index/egat/effects/realtime-effects) for
the stack as a whole.
