Passes frequencies below the cutoff and progressively attenuates everything
above it. Use it to take the edge off hiss, sibilance or other high-frequency
noise, or to darken a sound deliberately.

## Where to find it

**Effect → EQ and filters → Low-pass filter**.

## Controls

- **Frequency (Hz)** — the cutoff. Default 1000, minimum 0.
- **Roll-off (dB per octave)** — how steeply the level falls away above the
  cutoff: 6, 12, 24, 36 or 48 dB. Default 6 dB.

## Notes

The cutoff marks where attenuation begins rather than where sound stops. A 6
dB roll-off is a gentle tilt; 48 dB removes the top end much more decisively.

The effect has a preview, so you can compare settings before applying.
