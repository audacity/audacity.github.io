Cuts a narrow band around a single frequency while leaving everything either
side of it largely untouched. The usual job is mains hum — 50 Hz or 60 Hz
depending on where the recording was made — or a single whistling tone from
equipment.

## Where to find it

**Effect → EQ and filters → Notch filter**.

## Controls

- **Frequency (Hz)** — the centre of the band to remove. Default 60 Hz.
- **Q (higher value reduces width)** — 0.1 to 1000, default 1. Q sets how
  narrow the notch is: raise it to affect less of the surrounding audio, lower
  it to take out a wider band.

## Notes

Mains hum usually comes with harmonics, so removing the fundamental alone may
not be enough — you may need to apply the filter again at twice and three
times the frequency.

A high Q keeps the damage local but needs the frequency to be accurate; if
you are not sure of the exact frequency, start with a lower Q.
