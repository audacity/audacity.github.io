Ordinary effects act on a stretch of time and treat every frequency in it the
same way. Spectral tools act on a rectangle instead: a range of time *and* a
range of frequency, marked out by dragging in a spectrogram. That lets you
remove or adjust one sound — a whistle, a cough, a resonant ring — while
leaving everything around it in the same moment untouched.

## Where to find it

The **Spectral tools** submenu of the **Effect** menu.

## Making the selection

These effects need a spectral selection, so the track has to be showing a
spectrogram before you can make one — see
[Toggle Spectral View](/manual/manual-index/toolbar#toggle-spectral-view).
Drag in the spectrogram to select both a time range and a frequency range; the
effect then acts only on that rectangle.

## Notes

Several of the tools change what they do depending on how the selection is
made — with two frequency bounds they work on the band between them, with one
bound they filter everything above or below it. Each effect's own page says
which behaviour applies.
