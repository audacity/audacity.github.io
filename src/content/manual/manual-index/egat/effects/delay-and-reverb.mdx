Both effects in this group work by adding delayed copies of the signal to
itself. A delay does it with a handful of distinct repeats you can count; a
reverb does it with a dense wash of reflections you hear as a room rather than
as echoes.

## Where to find it

The **Delay and reverb** submenu of the **Effect** menu, with audio selected.

## Notes

These are the effects that make a close-miked, dead-sounding recording sit in a
place. A small amount is usually enough — if you can clearly hear the effect
as a separate thing, it is probably too much.

Delay and reverb both add sound after the end of the original, so leave some
silence at the end of the selection for the tail to occupy, or it will be cut
off.

Reverb can also be added to a track's effects panel and heard live during
playback — see
[Realtime Effects](/manual/manual-index/egat/effects/realtime-effects).
