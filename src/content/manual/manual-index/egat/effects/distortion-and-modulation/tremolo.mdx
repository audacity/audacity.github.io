Modulates the volume of the selection up and down at a steady rate, the
classic wobble of a guitar amplifier's tremolo circuit. Slow rates give a
gentle pulsing; fast rates with a square wave chop the sound into segments.

## Where to find it

**Effect → Distortion and modulation → Tremolo**.

## Controls

- **Waveform type** — Sine, Triangle, Sawtooth, Inverse Sawtooth or Square.
  Sine is the default.
- **Starting phase (degrees)** — −180 to 180, default 0. Where in the cycle
  the modulation begins, which decides whether the selection starts loud or
  quiet.
- **Wet level (percent)** — 1 to 100, default 40. How deep the modulation is:
  at 100 the sound drops to silence at the bottom of each cycle, at low
  values it barely dips.
- **Frequency (Hz)** — 0.001 to 1000, default 4. How many cycles per second.

## Notes

Sine and triangle give smooth swells; sawtooth and inverse sawtooth give a
ramp that resets abruptly; square switches between two levels. At high
frequencies the modulation stops being heard as a pulse and starts adding
sidebands, which is a way of getting ring-modulator-like sounds out of it.

The effect has a preview, so the rate and depth are easy to judge before
applying.
