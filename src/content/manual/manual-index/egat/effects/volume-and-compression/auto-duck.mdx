Auto Duck was an Audacity 3 effect that lowered one track automatically whenever a control track — typically a voice-over — rose above a threshold. **Audacity 4 does not ship it**, and there is no Auto Duck entry in the Effect menu.

## What to use instead

- **[Compressor](/manual/manual-index/egat/effects/volume-and-compression/compressor)** gives you threshold, ratio, attack and release, so you can pull a backing track down whenever it gets loud. It does not listen to a second track, so it is not a like-for-like replacement, but it covers the level-control half of the job.
- **[Clip envelope](/manual/manual-index/toolbar#clip-gain)** editing lets you draw the ducks by hand on the music clip, at the exact points where the voice-over starts and stops. For a handful of ducks this is usually quicker than setting up an automatic effect, and you keep full control over the shape of each one.
