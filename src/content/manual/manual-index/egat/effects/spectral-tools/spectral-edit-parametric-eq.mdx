Applies a single peaking EQ band to a spectral selection, centred on the
middle of the selected frequency range and as wide as the selection. Where
Amplify selection lifts or drops the whole band evenly, this shapes a peak or
a dip around one frequency, which suits narrow problems such as a single ring
or resonance.

## Where to find it

**Effect → Spectral tools → Amplify selection center frequency**.

## Making the selection

This effect works on a spectral selection, so the track must be showing a
spectrogram first — see
[Toggle Spectral View](/manual/manual-index/toolbar/toggle-spectral-view).
Drag in the spectrogram to select a time range and a frequency range. The
effect takes its centre frequency and its bandwidth from that selection.

## Controls

- **Gain (dB)** — −24 to 24, default 0. How much to boost or cut at the
  centre frequency. At 0 the effect does nothing.

## Notes

Because the bandwidth comes from the selection, a tall selection gives a
broad, gentle curve and a short one gives a narrow, surgical band.

A 10 ms fade at each end of the selection blends the processed region into
the audio around it.
