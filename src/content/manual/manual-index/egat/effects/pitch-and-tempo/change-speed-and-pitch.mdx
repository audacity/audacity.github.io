Change Speed was an Audacity 3 effect that resampled the selection, so speed and pitch moved together in the way a tape machine changes both at once. **Audacity 4 does not ship it**, and there is no Change Speed entry in the Effect menu.

## What to use instead

- **[Sliding stretch](/manual/manual-index/egat/effects/pitch-and-tempo/sliding-stretch)** changes tempo and pitch in one pass. To imitate the tape effect, move the tempo and pitch controls together rather than one alone; to slide the speed gradually across the selection, set different initial and final values.
- **[Change pitch](/manual/manual-index/egat/effects/pitch-and-tempo/change-pitch)** shifts pitch on its own, with the duration untouched.

Unlike Change Speed, both of these use time stretching rather than resampling, so pitch and duration are independent settings rather than two consequences of one control.
