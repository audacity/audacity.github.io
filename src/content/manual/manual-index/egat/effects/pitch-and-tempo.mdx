Pitch and tempo are separable in this group: you can raise the pitch without
speeding the audio up, fit a recording to a new length without changing the
key, or move both together the way a tape machine would. There is also a
stretch effect for pulling short sounds out into long textures.

## Where to find it

The **Pitch and tempo** submenu of the **Effect** menu, with audio selected.

## Notes

Changing pitch or tempo independently means resynthesising the audio, so large
moves leave audible artefacts — a smeared or watery quality on transients.
Small corrections are usually clean; anything drastic is a creative effect
rather than a repair.

Effects that change tempo or speed also change the length of the audio they
are applied to, so tracks that were lined up before may not be afterwards.
