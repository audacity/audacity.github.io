A fade changes level gradually across the whole of a selection rather than by a
fixed amount. The group covers the plain linear fades, shaped fades where you
choose the curve, and crossfades that take one piece of audio out while
bringing another in.

## Where to find it

The **Fading** submenu of the **Effect** menu, with audio selected.

## How fades use the selection

The fade always spans exactly the selection: it starts at its beginning and
finishes at its end. To change how long a fade lasts, change the length of the
selection, not a setting in a dialog.

## Notes

Fades are also the cure for a click at a cut point. A few milliseconds of fade
at the start and end of a clip removes the abrupt step in the waveform that
causes it.
