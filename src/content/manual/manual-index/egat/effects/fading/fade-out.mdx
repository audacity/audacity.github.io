Fade Out applies a linear fade to the selected audio: full level at the start
of the selection, falling evenly to silence at its end. Use it to end a piece
of music, to close a recording cleanly, or to remove the click at the tail of a
clip.

## Where to find it

**Effect → Fade Out**, with audio selected. There is no dialog — the fade is
applied as soon as you choose it.

## Notes

The fade always covers exactly the selection, so the length of the fade is set
by how much you select, not by a setting. Select five seconds and you get a
five-second fade.

The ramp is a straight line in amplitude. Because loudness is not linear, this
sounds like a slow start to the fade and then a rapid drop away at the very
end. For a fade that sounds more even, or one you can shape yourself, use
[Studio Fade Out](/manual/manual-index/egat/effects/fading/studio-fade-out) or
[Adjustable Fade](/manual/manual-index/egat/effects/fading/adjustable-fade).
