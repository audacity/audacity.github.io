Fade In applies a linear fade to the selected audio: silence at the start of
the selection, rising evenly to the original level at its end. Use it to bring
a piece of music in, or to cover the click at the head of a clip.

## Where to find it

**Effect → Fade In**, with audio selected. There is no dialog — the fade is
applied as soon as you choose it.

## Notes

The fade always covers exactly the selection, so the length of the fade is set
by how much you select, not by a setting. Select two seconds and you get a
two-second fade.

The ramp is a straight line in amplitude, which sounds like a fairly quick
start followed by a long approach to full level. For a curve you can shape
yourself, use
[Adjustable Fade](/manual/manual-index/egat/effects/fading/adjustable-fade).

Applying it twice compounds the two ramps rather than repeating one, giving a
much slower start.
