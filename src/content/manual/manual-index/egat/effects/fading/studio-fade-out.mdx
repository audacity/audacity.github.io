Fades the selection out with a smoother, more musical result than a plain
fade. As well as a sinusoidal level fade, it applies a low-pass filter that
sweeps down as the fade progresses — from the full spectrum at the start to
around 100 Hz at the end — so the sound darkens as it recedes rather than
simply getting quieter.

## Where to find it

**Effect → Fading → Studio fade out**.

There are no settings to adjust: the fade always runs across the whole
selection.

## Notes

The effect is destructive and is applied to the selected audio, so make the
selection cover exactly the stretch you want faded. For a fade whose shape
and end level you can control, use
[Adjustable Fade](/manual/manual-index/egat/effects/fading/adjustable-fade)
instead.
