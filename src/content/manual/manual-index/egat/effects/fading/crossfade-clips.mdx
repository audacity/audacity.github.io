Fades one clip out while fading the next one in, so that the join between
them is smooth rather than abrupt. It works within a single track, on two
clips that sit side by side.

## Where to find it

**Effect → Fading → Crossfade clips**.

## How to use it

Put the two clips in the same track, then select roughly the same amount of
audio from the end of the first clip and the start of the second. Applying
the effect crossfades the two selected regions. The clips do not have to be
touching — any gap between them is ignored.

There are no settings to adjust.

## Notes

If the selection is continuous audio with no split in it, the first and
second halves of the selection are crossfaded into each other instead. That
is a quick way to smooth over a discontinuity or a click in a waveform: make
a short selection across the glitch and apply the effect.

Two limits apply, though neither should come up in normal use. No more than
two clips may be selected in each channel, and the selection may not begin or
end in empty space.
