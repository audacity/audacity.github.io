Reverb simulates the reflections of a space, turning a close-miked, dead
recording into one that sounds as though it happened somewhere. It is built
around a room-size model rather than a specific measured space, so the controls
describe the room and how bright it is rather than naming a preset venue.

## Where to find it

**Effect → Delay and reverb → Reverb**, with audio selected. Reverb also works as a track effect:
add it from a track's effects panel to hear it live during playback without
altering the audio — see
[Realtime Effects](/manual/manual-index/egat/effects/realtime-effects).

## Controls

The three main settings are large knobs on the left; the rest are smaller knobs
alongside.

- **Room size** — how large the simulated space is, as a percentage. Range 0 to
  100, default 75.
- **Stereo width** — how far the reflections are spread across the stereo
  image, as a percentage. Range 0 to 100, default 100.
- **Pre-delay** — the gap between the dry sound and the first reflections, in
  ms. Range 0 to 200, default 10.
- **Damping** — how quickly high frequencies are absorbed as the reverb decays,
  as a percentage. Range 0 to 100, default 50.
- **Reverberance** — how long the reverb takes to die away, as a percentage.
  Range 0 to 100, default 50.
- **Low tone** — the amount of low frequency in the reverb, as a percentage.
  Range 0 to 100, default 100.
- **High tone** — the amount of high frequency in the reverb, as a percentage.
  Range 0 to 100, default 100.
- **Wet gain** — the level of the reverb itself, in dB. Range −20 to 10,
  default −1.
- **Dry gain** — the level of the original signal, in dB. Range −20 to 10,
  default −1.
- **Wet only** — output the reverb alone, with none of the dry signal. Off by
  default.

## Notes

**Pre-delay** is the control that keeps a voice intelligible in a large space:
a small gap before the reflections arrive separates the words from the wash
behind them.

**Wet only** is what you use to put the reverb on a track of its own, which you
can then balance, equalise or fade independently of the dry recording.

The reverb tail continues past the end of the original sound, so include some
silence at the end of the selection for it to occupy.

A set of factory presets ships with the effect and can be loaded from the
presets bar in the effect dialog.
