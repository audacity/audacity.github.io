**Echo is not among the effects that ship with Audacity 4.** It was a
separate effect in Audacity 3, offering a delay time and a decay factor.

Use [Delay](/manual/manual-index/egat/effects/delay-and-reverb/delay)
instead. It covers the same ground — a series of repeats at a set spacing,
each quieter than the last — and adds control over the number of repeats, the
way their spacing changes over time, and pitch shifting between them.

For a sense of space rather than distinct repeats, use
[Reverb](/manual/manual-index/egat/effects/delay-and-reverb/reverb).
