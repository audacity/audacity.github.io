Equalisers and filters lift or cut parts of the frequency range and leave the
rest alone. Reach for them to take rumble out of a voice recording, to tame a
harsh or boomy resonance, or to shape a whole mix so it sits comfortably.

## Where to find it

The **EQ and filters** submenu of the **Effect** menu, with audio selected.

## Choosing between them

The group spans two kinds of tool. The simple filters do one job each with two
or three settings: pass everything above or below a frequency, notch out a
single frequency, tilt one end of the spectrum. The equalisers give you the
whole curve at once — either as a row of fixed bands or as a curve you draw —
and are what you want when the correction is more than one move.

## Notes

Cutting is usually a better first instinct than boosting: removing what is in
the way costs no headroom, while boosting adds level that may then need
reining in.
