Everything in this group works on level. Some effects move the whole selection
up or down by a fixed amount; others change loud and quiet passages by
different amounts, narrowing the distance between them so that a track sits
more steadily in a mix or holds a consistent loudness from end to end.

## Where to find it

The **Volume and compression** submenu of the **Effect** menu, with audio
selected.

## Notes

Level effects are usually the last thing you apply, once the editing and
cleaning are done, because anything you do afterwards changes the peaks they
were working to.

The Compressor and the Limiter can also be added to a track's effects panel and
heard live during playback — see
[Realtime Effects](/manual/manual-index/egat/effects/realtime-effects).
