Repeat was an Audacity 3 effect that took the selection and appended a number
of copies of it, end to end. **Audacity 4 does not ship it**, and there is no
Repeat entry in the Effect menu.

## What to use instead

Copy and paste does the same job. Select the passage,
[Copy](/manual/manual-index/header/edit#copy) it, put the cursor at its end and
[Paste](/manual/manual-index/header/edit#paste) as many times as you want
copies. Because the clipboard keeps its contents, each paste costs one
keystroke, and you can stop when the length looks right rather than deciding
the number in advance.

[Duplicate](/manual/manual-index/header/edit#duplicate) is the other option
where you want the repeats on a track of their own rather than following the
original.

## Notes

Watch the joins. If the selection does not start and end at a zero crossing,
each repeat introduces a click at the seam — trim the selection to a musical
boundary, or apply a very short
[Fade In](/manual/manual-index/egat/effects/fading/fade-in) and
[Fade Out](/manual/manual-index/egat/effects/fading/fade-out) before copying.
