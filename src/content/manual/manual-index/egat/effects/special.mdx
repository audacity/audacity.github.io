The Special group collects the effects that do not belong to any of the sound-
shaping families. They are utilities: each does one exact thing to the
selection, most of them with no settings at all, and several are used as a step
in a larger technique rather than for their own sake.

## Where to find it

The **Special** submenu of the **Effect** menu, with audio selected.

## Notes

Because most of these effects have no dialog, they apply the moment you choose
them. If that was not what you wanted, undo.
