A generator writes audio from nothing — a tone, a noise, a rhythm, a plucked
string, or an exactly flat stretch of silence. Generators are how you make test
signals, tuning references, click tracks and timing gaps without recording
anything.

## Where to find them

The **Generate** menu.

## How generation works

Generation starts at the beginning of the selection, or at the cursor if
nothing is selected. Most generators ask for a duration and write that much
audio from that point.

## Notes

Some generators are built into Audacity and some are
[Nyquist](/manual/manual-index/egat/tools/nyquist) plug-ins; they sit together in the
same menu.
