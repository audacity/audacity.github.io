The timeline is the ruler running along the top of the tracks area. It shows
the project's time scale, carries the playhead, and holds the loop region.
The grid lines drawn behind the tracks follow the same ticks as the ruler, so
what you see on the ruler is what you see across the waveforms.

## Reading the ruler

The ruler can label time in two ways:
[Minutes & seconds](/manual/manual-index/timeline/minutes-and-seconds) for
clock time, or
[Beats & measures](/manual/manual-index/timeline/beats-and-measures) for
musical time. Either way, the tick spacing changes as you zoom, so the
labels stay readable at every zoom level.

## Using the ruler

The ruler is split into two halves, top and bottom, which behave differently:

- **Lower half** — click to move the playhead to that point. Whether
  playback also starts there is controlled by
  [Click ruler to start playback](/manual/manual-index/timeline/click-ruler-to-start-playback).
- **Upper half** — the loop region strip. When no loop region exists, hold
  Ctrl and drag here to draw one; drawing a region turns looping on. Once a
  region exists, drag either edge to resize it, drag its middle to move it,
  or click inside it without dragging to turn looping off and on. Dragging follows
  the current [snapping](/manual/manual-index/toolbar/snapping) setting, and
  a guideline shows where the edge will land.

Right-click anywhere on the ruler for the timeline options below.

## Ruler options

The right-click menu holds four groups of options:

- The ruler format — **Minutes & seconds** or **Beats & measures**.
- How the view behaves during playback — **Update display while playing**,
  **Pinned playhead** and **Click ruler to start playback**.
- The loop region — **Toggle loop region**, **Clear loop region**, **Set loop
  region to selection**, **Set selection to loop** and **Creating a loop also
  selects audio**.
- **Show vertical rulers**, which turns on the per-track amplitude scale at
  the right-hand edge of the tracks area.
