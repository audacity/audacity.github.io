**Project** is the editing view: the tracks, the toolbars and everything you
use to work on audio. Switching to it from Home puts the project you have
open back in front of you.

## Where to find it

The **Project** tab, next to **Home** at the top left of the window. It stays
greyed out until a project is open, and its label is shown in bold while one
is.

## What the view holds

- The bar across the top: the Home and Project tabs, the
  [Hotbar](/manual/manual-index/hotbar), the workspace selector and
  Undo/Redo.
- The [Transport Toolbar](/manual/manual-index/toolbar), with the playback,
  recording and editing controls.
- The [Timeline](/manual/manual-index/timeline) and the track area.
- The [Track Control Panel](/manual/manual-index/track-control-panel) at the
  left of each track.
- A status bar along the bottom showing the current selection.

Two more panels are hidden until you ask for them: **History**, from
[Show history](/manual/manual-index/header/view/show-history), and the
playback meter panel, which the
[Modern](/manual/manual-index/workspaces/modern) workspace uses in place of
the meters in the toolbar.

Which of these appear, and which buttons the Transport Toolbar carries,
depends on the [workspace](/manual/manual-index/workspaces) you are using.
