Modern is the workspace Audacity 4 starts with. It is described at first
launch as "a clearer interface, ideal for new users": the toolbar carries
only the controls needed to record, play and look around a project, and the
clipboard and trimming commands are left to the menus and keyboard.

## What the toolbar shows

- Play
- Stop
- Record
- Rewind to Start
- Rewind to End
- Toggle Loop Region
- Clip Gain
- Split Tool
- Toggle Spectral View
- Zoom In
- Zoom Out
- Timecode
- Snapping
- Record Level
- Playback Level

Cut, Copy, Paste, Trim, Silence and the three extra zoom buttons (Zoom to
Selection, Zoom to Fit Project and Zoom Toggle) are hidden. They all still
work from the Header menus and their keyboard shortcuts.

## Level meters

Modern is the only shipped workspace that moves the playback meter out of
the toolbar and into a panel down the side of the window.

## Ruler

The Timeline ruler is set to Minutes and Seconds.
