**Edit workspaces** opens a window listing every workspace you have,
built-in and your own, with a short explanation that workspaces let you
choose different configurations of the Audacity interface.

## Where to find it

From the workspace dropdown in the top right corner of the window, or from
**View → Workspaces**.

## What you can do

- **Switch** to any workspace in the list.
- **Save layout as new workspace** — see
  [Creating New Workspaces](/manual/manual-index/workspaces/creating-new-workspaces).
- **Remove** a workspace you created. The three built-in workspaces —
  Classic, Modern and Music — cannot be removed.
- **Reset** a built-in workspace to its factory layout. Audacity warns that
  this cannot be undone before it goes ahead.

## Notes

- A workspace remembers which toolbar buttons are shown and how the window
  is laid out. It does not remember global appearance settings such as the
  theme, accent colour or waveform display options — those stay the same
  whichever workspace you switch to.
- **Tools → Reset configuration** goes further: it clears app preferences
  and custom UI configurations, and deletes custom workspaces and
  shortcuts. It does not touch your projects.
