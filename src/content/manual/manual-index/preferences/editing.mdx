The **Audio editing** page decides how editing commands behave.

## What it covers

- **Paste behaviour** — what happens to surrounding audio when you paste.
- **Delete behaviour** — whether deleting leaves a gap or closes it.
- **Effect behaviour** — how effects apply to a selection.
- **Mono/stereo conversion** — what happens when audio of one channel
  count is pasted into a track of another.
- **Asymmetric stereo heights** — whether the two channels of a stereo
  track can be resized independently.
- **Zoom toggle** — the two zoom levels that Zoom Toggle switches between.
