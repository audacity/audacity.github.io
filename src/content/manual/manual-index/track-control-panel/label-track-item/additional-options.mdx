A label track's menu is opened the same way as any other track's: with the
menu button beside the track name, by right-clicking the control panel, or —
with the track focused — with <Shortcut client:load keys="shift+f10" />.

It is much shorter than an audio track's menu, and holds only:

- **Rename**, **Duplicate** and **Delete**
- **Move track** — the four move commands
- **Track color** — the nine track colours
- **Export labels**

## Notes

Because a label track carries no audio, the audio-only entries are absent:
there is no Track visualization, no Show vertical rulers, no Meters &
monitoring, no channel commands, and no Format, Rate or Resample.
