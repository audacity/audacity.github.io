**Add track** sits at the right of the **Tracks** title bar, above the track
control panels. Clicking it opens a small popup offering three track types:

- **Mono** — a single-channel audio track.
- **Stereo** — a two-channel audio track.
- **Label** — a track that holds labels rather than audio.

Choosing one creates the track immediately and closes the popup. Clicking
**Add track** again while the popup is open closes it.

## Notes

The same three track types can be created from the Tracks menu:
[New mono track](/manual/manual-index/header/tracks/new-mono-track) — which
also has the default shortcut <Shortcut client:load keys="cmd+shift+n" /> —
[New stereo track](/manual/manual-index/header/tracks/new-stereo-track) and
[New label track](/manual/manual-index/header/tracks/new-label-track).
