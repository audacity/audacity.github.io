The volume slider sets how loud an audio track plays back, relative to the
audio it contains. It runs from **-60 dB** to **+12 dB**, with 0 dB — no
change — in the middle of that range. A tooltip follows the handle while you
drag, showing the current value in dB.

## Using it

- Drag the handle, or click anywhere along the slider, to set a value.
- Hold **Shift** while dragging for fine adjustment; the slider then moves far
  more slowly than the pointer.
- Double-click the slider to reset the track to 0 dB.
- With the slider focused, the up and down keys step the value.

## Notes

The slider is hidden while the track is collapsed. It affects playback only —
the audio in the track is left untouched.
