**Effects** is a full-width button across the bottom of an audio track's
control panel. It opens the effects panel for that track, where its chain of
realtime effects is built and managed.

The panel shows the track's name with a power button beside it that bypasses
or re-enables the whole chain at once, the list of effects currently on the
track, and an **Add effect** button that opens the list of available effects.
Individual effects in the list can be bypassed, reordered and removed.

## Notes

Realtime effects are non-destructive: they are applied as the track plays, so
they can be changed or removed at any time without altering the audio.

The button is hidden while the track is collapsed, or when the track is too
short for it to fit.

See [Using realtime effects](/manual/audio-editing/using-realtime-effects) for
a fuller walkthrough.
