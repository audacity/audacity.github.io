Every audio track has a menu holding the options that do not fit on the
control panel itself. Open it with the menu button beside the track name, by
right-clicking anywhere on the control panel, or — with the track focused —
with <Shortcut client:load keys="shift+f10" />.

The menu contains, in order:

- **Rename**, **Duplicate** and **Delete**
- **Move track** — a submenu of the four move commands
- **Track visualization** — waveform, spectrogram and half-wave options
- **Track color** — the nine track colours
- **Show vertical rulers**
- **Meters & monitoring**
- The channel commands: **Swap stereo channels**, **Split stereo to L/R mono**
  and **Split stereo to center mono** on a stereo track, or **Make stereo
  track** on a mono one
- **Format:** and **Rate:**, each showing the track's current setting in its
  own title, and **Resample track…**

## Notes

**Make stereo track** appears only on mono tracks, and is available only when
exactly one track is selected and the track directly below it is also mono. It
combines the two into a single stereo track.
