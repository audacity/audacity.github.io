**Split stereo to center mono** turns a stereo track into two separate mono
tracks, one carrying the left channel and one the right, with both panned to
the centre. Each channel then plays through both speakers and can be edited,
levelled and processed independently.

## Where to find it

In the track menu of a stereo track, the last of the three channel commands.
It does not appear on mono or label tracks.

## Notes

Because both tracks come out centred, the stereo image of the original
recording is lost. To keep it, use
[Split stereo to L/R mono](/manual/manual-index/track-control-panel/audio-track-item/additional-options/split-stereo-to-l-r-mono)
instead.
