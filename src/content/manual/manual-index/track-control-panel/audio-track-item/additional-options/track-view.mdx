In the current Audacity 4 alpha this submenu is called **Track
visualization**. It sets how the selected track draws its audio, and holds:

- **Waveform** — the familiar amplitude view.
- **Spectrogram** — frequency content over time.
- **Multi-view** — waveform and spectrogram together, stacked in the one
  track.
- **Half-wave** — draw only the upper half of the waveform.
- **Spectrogram settings…** — opens the spectrogram settings for this track.

## Notes

Waveform, Spectrogram and Multi-view are mutually exclusive; the one in use is
ticked. Half-wave is a separate toggle that can be on alongside any of them.

The choice applies to this track only.
