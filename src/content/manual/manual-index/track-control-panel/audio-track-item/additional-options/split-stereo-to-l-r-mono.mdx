**Split stereo to L/R mono** turns a stereo track into two separate mono
tracks, one carrying the left channel and one the right. The two are panned
hard left and hard right, so playback still sounds as it did before the split
— but each channel can now be edited, and given its own volume, effects and
mute or solo state, on its own.

## Where to find it

In the track menu of a stereo track, directly below **Swap stereo channels**.
The command does not appear on mono or label tracks.

## Notes

If you want the two channels panned centre instead, use
[Split stereo to center mono](/manual/manual-index/track-control-panel/audio-track-item/additional-options/split-stereo-to-center-mono).
