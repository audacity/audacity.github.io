The last group of the track menu holds three items that deal with how a
track's audio is stored.

**Format:** is a submenu offering **16-bit PCM**, **24-bit PCM** and **32-bit
Float**. The one in use is ticked, and the submenu's own title shows it — for
example *Format: 32-bit Float*.

**Rate:** is a submenu listing the sample rates your current audio device
supports, plus **Other…** for a rate that is not on the list. As with Format,
the title shows the track's current rate — for example *Rate: 44100 Hz* — and
the rate in use is ticked. If the track is at a rate the device does not
offer, **Other…** is the item shown as ticked.

**Resample track…** converts the audio in the track to a different sample
rate.

## Notes

Format and Rate are both disabled while a recording is in progress.
