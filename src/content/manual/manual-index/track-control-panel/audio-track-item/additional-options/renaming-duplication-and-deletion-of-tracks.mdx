The track menu opens with three commands that act on the whole track.

- **Rename** puts the track's name into edit mode in the control panel. Type
  the new name and confirm it.
- **Duplicate** makes a copy of the track, with its audio, and adds it to the
  project.
- **Delete** removes the track and everything in it from the project.

## Notes

Renaming can also be started without the menu: double-click the track name in
the control panel.

All three are undoable.
