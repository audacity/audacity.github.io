A narrow vertical meter runs down the right-hand edge of every audio track's
control panel, showing the level leaving that track while it plays or records.
A mono track has one bar; a stereo track has two side by side, the left
channel first.

Each bar shows the current peak level with an RMS reading drawn over it, plus
a marker holding the highest peak reached. If the signal clips, the bar
switches to its clipped colour and stays there until the meter is cleared.

## Clearing the meters

Click the meters to clear them, which resets both the peak-hold marker and the
clipping indication. They are also cleared automatically when playback or
recording starts, and when the track loses focus.

## Notes

The meters read in dBFS, loudest at the top. A muted track shows no level.
