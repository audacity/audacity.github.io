Keeps the selected audio and removes everything outside it, trimming the clip
down to the selection.

<UIExample component="tool-button" variant="trim" />

## Trim and Silence

The two sit together because they are opposites: Trim keeps the selection and
discards the rest, [Silence](/manual/manual-index/toolbar/silence) keeps the
rest and empties the selection.

## Where to find it

In the last tools group, next to Silence. It appears in the Classic, Modern
and Music workspaces; the Spectral editing workspace leaves it out.
