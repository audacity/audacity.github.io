Turns on envelope editing, so you can draw volume changes across a clip
rather than setting one level for the whole thing. The button is labelled
**Clip envelope** in the toolbar.

<UIExample component="toggle-tool-button" variant="envelope" />

It is a toggle: it stays pressed while envelope editing is on, and clicking
it again returns you to normal editing.

<UIExample component="toggle-tool-button" variant="envelope-on" />

## Where to find it

First button in the tools group, immediately after the transport controls.
It appears in the Classic, Modern and Music workspaces; the Spectral editing
workspace leaves it out.

<Callout type="tip">
  A clip's envelope is drawn on the clip itself, so you can see the shape you
  have made without leaving the track.
</Callout>
