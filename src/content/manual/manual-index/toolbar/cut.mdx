Removes the selected audio and puts it on the clipboard, ready to paste
elsewhere.

<UIExample component="tool-button" variant="cut" />

## Where to find it

First button in the clipboard group, alongside Copy and Paste. The clipboard
group appears in the Classic workspace only — the other workspaces leave
these three out.
