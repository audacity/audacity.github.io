Controls whether edits snap to a grid, and how fine that grid is. Two
controls sit side by side in the Transport Toolbar: a **Snap** mode selector
and a subdivision selector.

## The two controls

- **Snap** — whether snapping applies at all, and what it snaps to.
- **Subdivision** — how fine the grid is when snapping is on.

With snapping on, dragging a clip, moving a selection edge or positioning the
cursor lands on the nearest grid line rather than an arbitrary point. That's
what makes edits line up with a beat or a whole second instead of landing a
few samples out.

## Which grid

The grid follows the timeline's current format. Working in minutes and
seconds, snapping targets time divisions; working in beats and measures, it
targets musical divisions and respects the project tempo and time signature.
See [Tempo](/manual/manual-index/toolbar/tempo) and
[Time Signature](/manual/manual-index/toolbar/time-signature) for those, and
the Timeline pages for switching the ruler between the two formats.

## Where to find it

To the right of the timecode display, before the level meters.
