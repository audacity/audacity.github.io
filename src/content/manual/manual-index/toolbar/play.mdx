Starts playback from the edit cursor, or from the start of the selection if
you have one.

<UIExample component="transport-button" variant="play" />

Once playback is running the same button becomes Pause — press it to stop at
the current position, and again to carry on from there.

<UIExample component="transport-button" variant="playing" />

## What it plays

- **No selection** — plays from the edit cursor to the end of the project.
- **A selection** — plays the selected region only.
- **A loop region enabled** — plays the loop region and repeats it. See
  [Toggle Loop Region](/manual/manual-index/toolbar/toggle-loop-region).

Playback follows whichever tracks are unmuted. A soloed track plays on its
own; see the Track Control Panel for mute and solo.

## Where to find it

First button in the transport group, at the left of the Transport Toolbar.
The group runs Play, Stop, Record, Rewind to Start, Rewind to End, then
Toggle Loop Region.

<Callout type="tip">
  The Transport Toolbar can be moved — dragged to the top, the bottom, or left
  floating. If you can't find these buttons, check the other edge of the
  window.
</Callout>
