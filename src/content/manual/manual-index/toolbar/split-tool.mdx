Turns on the split tool. While it is on, clicking a clip splits it at that
point instead of selecting it. The button is labelled **Cut / Split** in the
toolbar.

<UIExample component="toggle-tool-button" variant="split" />

It is a toggle and stays pressed while the tool is active:

<UIExample component="toggle-tool-button" variant="split-on" />

The current design gives it the **S** key.

## Where to find it

In the tools group, next to Clip envelope. It appears in the Classic, Modern
and Music workspaces.
