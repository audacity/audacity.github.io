Moves the playhead backwards. In the Transport Toolbar the button is
labelled **Step backward** and carries a skip-back icon.

<UIExample component="transport-button" variant="step-backward" />

The button is unavailable while playback is running — stop first, then step.

## Where to find it

In the transport group, after Record and before Step forward. It is present
in every workspace.
