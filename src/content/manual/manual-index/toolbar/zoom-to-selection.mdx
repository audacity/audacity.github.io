Zooms so the current selection fills the visible timeline. The button is
labelled **Fit selection** in the toolbar.

<UIExample component="tool-button" variant="fit-selection" />

It needs a selection to work on — see the Selection Toolbar at the bottom of
the window for the current selection's start, end and duration.

## Where to find it

In the zoom group, after Zoom out. It appears in the Classic workspace only;
the Modern, Music and Spectral editing workspaces carry the shorter zoom
group of Zoom in, Zoom out and Zoom toggle.
