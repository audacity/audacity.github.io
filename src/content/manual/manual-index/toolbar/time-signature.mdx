Sets the project time signature — how many beats are in a bar, and which note
value counts as a beat. It is a selector: pick a signature from the list.

Together with [Tempo](/manual/manual-index/toolbar/tempo), it decides where
bar and beat lines fall, which in turn drives the `beats:bars`
[timecode](/manual/manual-index/toolbar/timecode) format and the musical
subdivisions in [Snapping](/manual/manual-index/toolbar/snapping).

## Where to find it

Next to Tempo, after the timecode. Like Tempo, it appears in the **Music**
workspace only.
