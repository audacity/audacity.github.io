Switches the track display from a waveform to a spectrogram, which shows
frequency against time instead of amplitude against time. The button is
labelled **Spectral view** in the toolbar.

<UIExample component="toggle-tool-button" variant="spectral" />

It is a toggle and stays pressed while spectral view is on:

<UIExample component="toggle-tool-button" variant="spectral-on" />

## In the Spectral editing workspace

That workspace is already in spectral view, so instead of a Spectral view
button it offers the reverse — a **Waveform** button that takes you back:

<UIExample component="toggle-tool-button" variant="waveform" />

## Where to find it

In the tools group. It appears in the Classic and Modern workspaces; the
Music workspace leaves it out, and Spectral editing replaces it with the
Waveform toggle described above.
