Sets the project tempo in beats per minute. It is a stepper: type a value, or
use the up and down controls to nudge it.

Tempo is what makes musical positions meaningful — it drives the
`beats:bars` [timecode](/manual/manual-index/toolbar/timecode) format and
musical [snapping](/manual/manual-index/toolbar/snapping).

## Where to find it

After the timecode. Tempo appears in the **Music** workspace only — the
Classic, Modern and Spectral editing workspaces do not show it. See
[Workspaces](/manual/manual-index/workspaces) for switching between them.
