The **Effect** menu is where you apply processing — EQ, compression, reverb
and so on — to the audio you have selected, and where you manage the plugins
that provide those effects.

## What it contains

- [Plugin manager](/manual/manual-index/header/effect/plugin-manager) —
  enable and disable effect plugins.
- [Add realtime effects](/manual/manual-index/header/effect/add-realtime-effects) —
  show the realtime effects panel.
- [Repeat last effect](/manual/manual-index/header/effect/repeat-last-effect) —
  run the most recent effect again with the same settings.
- Below a separator, the list of effect processors. This list is populated
  from your installed plugins, so its exact contents depend on what is
  installed on your machine.

The individual effects are documented in the
[Effects](/manual/manual-index/egat/effects) section of this manual.
