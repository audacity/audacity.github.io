The **Generate** menu creates new audio rather than changing audio you already
have — tones, noise, silence, rhythm tracks and similar material.

## What it contains

- [Plugin manager](/manual/manual-index/header/effect/plugin-manager) — enable
  and disable the plugins that provide generators.
- Below a separator, the list of available generators. This list is populated
  from your installed plugins, so its exact contents depend on what is
  installed on your machine — the built-in set includes Noise, Silence and
  DTMF Tones, plus Nyquist generators such as Pluck, Rhythm track and
  Risset drum.

The individual generators are documented in the
[Generators](/manual/manual-index/egat/generators) section of this manual.
