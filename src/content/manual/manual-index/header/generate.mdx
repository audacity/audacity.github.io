The **Generate** menu creates new audio rather than changing audio you already
have — tones, noise, silence, rhythm tracks and similar material.

Below **Plugin manager** and a separator, the menu lists the available
generators. That list is populated from your installed plugins, so its exact
contents depend on what is installed on your machine — the built-in set
includes Noise, Silence and DTMF Tones, plus Nyquist generators such as
Pluck, Rhythm track and Risset drum. The individual generators are documented
in the [Generators](/manual/manual-index/egat/generators) section of this
manual.

## Plugin manager

Enable and disable the plugins that provide generators — the same dialog as
[Plugin manager](/manual/manual-index/header/effect#plugin-manager) on the
Effect menu.
