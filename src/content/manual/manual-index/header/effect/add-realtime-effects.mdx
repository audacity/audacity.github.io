Shows the realtime effects panel, where you add effects that process a track
live during playback without changing the recorded audio. Choosing the
command again hides the panel.

## Where to find it

**Effect** menu, or press <Shortcut client:load keys="e" />.

## Notes

Realtime effects are non-destructive — they run on the track's output rather
than rewriting its audio, so you can change or remove them at any time. See
[Realtime effects](/manual/manual-index/egat/effects/realtime-effects) for
how the panel works.
