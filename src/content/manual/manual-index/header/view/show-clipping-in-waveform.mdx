Toggles whether clipping — audio that reaches or exceeds full scale — is
marked in the waveform display. It only changes how the waveform is drawn;
the audio itself is untouched.

The setting applies to the whole application, not per project.

## Where to find it

**View → Show clipping in waveform**. The menu item is checked while the
option is on.
