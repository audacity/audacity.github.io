Switches between two preset zoom levels, jumping to whichever of the two is
further from your current zoom — so you can flip between a close-up view
and a wider one without stepping through Zoom in and Zoom out. The same
command is a toolbar button — see
[Zoom Toggle](/manual/manual-index/toolbar/zoom-toggle).

## Where to find it

**View → Zoom → Zoom toggle**, or press <Shortcut client:load keys="shift+z" />.
