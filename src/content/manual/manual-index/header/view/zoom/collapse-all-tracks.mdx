Reduces every track in the project to its collapsed height, so more tracks
fit on screen at once. Use
[Expand all tracks](/manual/manual-index/header/view/zoom/expand-all-tracks)
to bring them back to full height.

## Where to find it

**View → Zoom → Collapse all tracks**, or press
<Shortcut client:load keys="cmd+shift+c" />.
