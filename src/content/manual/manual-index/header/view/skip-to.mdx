**Not yet enabled in the current Audacity 4 alpha.**

The **Skip to** submenu will hold two commands for moving the playback
position without playing: skip to the selection's start, and skip to its
end.

## Where to find it

**View → Skip to**. The submenu is present in the menu but greyed out in
this alpha.
