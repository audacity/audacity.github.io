The **Zoom** submenu controls how much of the timeline you see at once:
zooming in and out, returning to the default zoom, fitting the selection or
the whole project to the window, toggling between two zoom levels, and
collapsing or expanding all tracks.

When zooming, Audacity keeps the view anchored sensibly: with an active
selection it zooms around the selection's centre, otherwise around the
playback position.

The pages below cover each command in the submenu.

## Where to find it

**View → Zoom**. Several of these commands are also buttons in the
toolbar's zoom group — see the Toolbar section.
