The **Help** menu links out to learning resources, checks for new versions,
connects your audio.com account, and holds recovery commands for when the
application's configuration needs a clean start.

## What it contains

- [Check for updates](/manual/manual-index/header/help/check-update) — see
  whether a newer Audacity is available. Only shown in builds that support
  in-app updates.
- [Tutorials](/manual/manual-index/header/help/tutorials) — opens learning
  material on the web.
- [Online handbook](/manual/manual-index/header/help/online-handbook) —
  opens this manual in your web browser.
- [Diagnostics](/manual/manual-index/header/help/diagnostics) — a submenu of
  troubleshooting and developer commands (not yet enabled in the current
  alpha).
- [Link account](/manual/manual-index/header/help/link-account) — connect
  Audacity to your audio.com account.
- [About Audacity](/manual/manual-index/header/help/about-audacity) —
  version, credits and legal information. An **About Qt** item alongside it
  shows the same for the Qt toolkit Audacity is built on.
- [Revert to factory settings](/manual/manual-index/header/help/revert-factory) —
  reset the application to a fresh install's state.
