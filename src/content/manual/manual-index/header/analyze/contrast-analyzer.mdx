Compares the volume of two selections — typically speech in the foreground
against the background behind it — and reports the difference between them.
It exists to check that spoken content is loud enough relative to its
background, as accessibility guidelines require.

## Where to find it

**Analyze** menu, or press <Shortcut client:load keys="cmd+shift+t" />.

## Notes

In the current Audacity 4 alpha the menu item is present but the analyzer
window itself has not yet been implemented.
