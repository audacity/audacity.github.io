Copies the selected audio into a new track at the same position on the
timeline, leaving the original untouched. Useful when you want to experiment
with an edit or effect while keeping the original version to hand.

## Where to find it

**Edit → Duplicate**, or press <Shortcut client:load keys="cmd+d" />.

## Notes

What gets duplicated depends on the selection:

- With clips selected, those clips are duplicated.
- With a time selection, the selected range of each selected track is
  duplicated.
- With whole tracks selected and no time selection, the entire tracks are
  duplicated.
