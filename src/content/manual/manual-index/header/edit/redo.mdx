Reapplies the edit you most recently undid. Like
[Undo](/manual/manual-index/header/edit/undo), it can be pressed repeatedly
to step forward through several undone edits.

## Where to find it

**Edit → Redo**, or press <Shortcut client:load keys="cmd+shift+z" />.

## Notes

Redo is only available directly after undoing. Making a new edit discards
the undone steps, so there is nothing left to redo.
