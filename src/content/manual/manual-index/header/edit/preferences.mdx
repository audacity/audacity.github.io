Opens the Preferences dialog, where the application's settings live —
appearance, audio devices, editing behaviour, playback and recording,
spectral display, cloud, keyboard shortcuts and plugins.

## Where to find it

**Edit → Preferences**. On macOS it appears under the application menu
instead, following the platform convention.

## Notes

Preferences apply to Audacity as a whole rather than to the open project,
so changes persist between sessions. Shortcuts can be reassigned from the
Shortcuts page — the defaults are listed in the
[keyboard shortcuts reference](/manual/shortcuts).
