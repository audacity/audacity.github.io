Collects the selected clips into a group, so they are treated as one unit
when you select and move clips. The clips themselves are unchanged — they
keep their own names, positions and tracks.

## Where to find it

**Edit → Clip → Group clips**, or press <Shortcut client:load keys="cmd+g" />.

## Notes

To dissolve a group and work with its clips individually again, use
[Ungroup Clips](/manual/manual-index/header/edit/clip/ungroup-clips).
