Trims the clip down to the selection: the audio inside the selection is
kept, and the rest of the clip is removed. The same operation is available
as the [Trim](/manual/manual-index/toolbar/trim) toolbar button.

## Where to find it

**Edit → Clip → Trim clip**, or press <Shortcut client:load keys="cmd+t" />.

## Notes

Trim is the opposite of
[Silence Audio](/manual/manual-index/header/edit/silence-audio): Trim keeps
the selection and discards the rest, Silence keeps the rest and empties the
selection.
