Dissolves the group that the selected clips belong to, so each clip can be
selected, moved and edited on its own again. The clips themselves are
unchanged — only the grouping is removed.

## Where to find it

**Edit → Clip → Ungroup clips**, or press
<Shortcut client:load keys="cmd+shift+g" />.

## Notes

The counterpart of
[Group Clips](/manual/manual-index/header/edit/clip/group-clips).
