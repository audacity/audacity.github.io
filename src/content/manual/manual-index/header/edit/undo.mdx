Reverses your most recent edit. Press it again to step further back —
Audacity keeps a history of your edits for the current session, so you can
undo a whole series of changes one step at a time.

## Where to find it

**Edit → Undo**, or press <Shortcut client:load keys="cmd+z" />.

## Notes

The command is only available once there is something to undo. If you go
back too far, [Redo](/manual/manual-index/header/edit/redo) steps forward
again.
