The **Tracks** menu covers the tracks themselves rather than the audio
inside them: adding new tracks to record or paste into, duplicating and
removing existing ones, and mixing several tracks down into one.

Commands here act on the tracks you have selected. Selecting a track is
different from selecting a time range — see
[Select all tracks](/manual/manual-index/header/select/select-all-tracks).

The pages below cover each command in the menu.
