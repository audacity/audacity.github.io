The **Analyze** menu inspects your audio without changing it — measuring
levels, finding features, or displaying frequency content.

Below a separator, the menu lists the available analyzers. That list is
populated from your installed plugins, so its exact contents depend on what is
installed on your machine — the built-in Nyquist set includes Beat finder,
Label sounds and Measure RMS. The individual analyzers are documented in the
[Analyzers](/manual/manual-index/egat/analyzers) section of this manual.

## Plugin manager

Enable and disable the plugins that provide analyzers — the same dialog as
[Plugin manager](/manual/manual-index/header/effect#plugin-manager) on the
Effect menu.

## Contrast analyzer

Compares the volume of two selections — typically speech in the foreground
against the background behind it — and reports the difference between them.
It exists to check that spoken content is loud enough relative to its
background, as accessibility guidelines require. Shortcut:
<Shortcut client:load keys="cmd+shift+t" />.

In the current Audacity 4 alpha the menu item is present but the analyzer
window itself has not yet been implemented.

## Plot spectrum

Analyses the selected audio and plots how much energy it contains at each
frequency — a snapshot of the selection's tonal content, useful for spotting
hum, resonances or how bright or dark a recording is overall.

In the current Audacity 4 alpha the menu item is present but the spectrum
window itself has not yet been implemented.
