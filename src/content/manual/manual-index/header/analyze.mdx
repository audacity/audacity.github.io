The **Analyze** menu inspects your audio without changing it — measuring
levels, finding features, or displaying frequency content.

## What it contains

- [Plugin manager](/manual/manual-index/header/effect/plugin-manager) —
  enable and disable the plugins that provide analyzers.
- [Contrast analyzer](/manual/manual-index/header/analyze/contrast-analyzer) —
  compare the loudness of two selections.
- [Plot spectrum](/manual/manual-index/header/analyze/plot-spectrum) — show
  the frequency content of a selection.
- Below a separator, the list of available analyzers. This list is populated
  from your installed plugins, so its exact contents depend on what is
  installed on your machine — the built-in Nyquist set includes Beat finder,
  Label sounds and Measure RMS.

The individual analyzers are documented in the
[Analyzers](/manual/manual-index/egat/analyzers) section of this manual.
