Saves the current project. A project that already lives on your computer is
saved in place; a cloud project is saved and synced to the cloud. The first
time you save a brand-new project, Audacity asks where it should live — on
your computer or in the cloud.

## Where to find it

**File → Save**, or press <Shortcut client:load keys="cmd+s" />.

## Notes

- The choice dialog for new projects includes an option to stop asking, so
  future new projects go straight to your preferred location.
- Saving is unavailable while recording.
