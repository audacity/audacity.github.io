Opens the Export dialog, where you turn the project into an ordinary audio
file that other applications can play. Exporting produces a rendered copy —
your project, with its separate tracks and clips, is unaffected.

## Where to find it

**File → Export audio…**, or press <Shortcut client:load keys="cmd+shift+e" />.

## Notes

- Greyed out until the project contains some audio, and unavailable while
  recording.
