**Not yet enabled in the current Audacity 4 alpha.**

The **Spectral** submenu will hold the toggle for spectral selection —
selecting a frequency range as well as a time range, so edits can target
part of the spectrum rather than the whole waveform.

## Where to find it

**Select → Spectral**. The submenu is present in the menu but greyed out in
this alpha.
