Selects a region that ends at the current playback position. A small
**Playback position** dialog asks for a time; the selection then runs from
that time up to the playback position. If the time you enter is at or after
the playback position, nothing is selected.

## Where to find it

**Select → Region → Left of playback position**, or press
<Shortcut client:load keys="[" />.
