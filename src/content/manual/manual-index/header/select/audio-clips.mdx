**Not yet enabled in the current Audacity 4 alpha.**

The **Audio clips** submenu will hold selection commands that work in terms
of clips rather than times: extending the selection to the previous or next
clip boundary, and selecting the previous or next clip outright.

## Where to find it

**Select → Audio clips**. The submenu is present in the menu but greyed out
in this alpha.
