**Not yet enabled in the current Audacity 4 alpha.**

A submenu with two commands for reordering the tracks in the project: by
the time their audio starts, or alphabetically by track name.

## Where to find it

**Tracks → Sort tracks**. The submenu is present in the menu bar but greyed
out in the current alpha.

## Notes

Tracks can be reordered by hand in the meantime by dragging them in the
[track control panel](/manual/manual-index/track-control-panel).
