**Not yet enabled in the current Audacity 4 alpha.**

A submenu of commands for lining up the content of selected tracks:
aligning clips end to end, aligning them together, or aligning their start
or end to zero, to the playhead, or to the end of the selection.

## Where to find it

**Tracks → Align content**. The submenu is present in the menu bar but
greyed out in the current alpha.

## Notes

Until these commands are enabled you can position clips by dragging them,
with snapping on to line them up precisely — see
[Snapping](/manual/manual-index/toolbar/snapping).
