Combines the selected tracks into one, mixing their audio together as
playback would. The result replaces the separate tracks with a single mono
or stereo track.

## Where to find it

**Tracks → Mix-down to…**.

## Notes

Mixing down bakes in the current volume, pan and effect settings of each
track, so it is worth duplicating the tracks first if you may want to
adjust the balance later. Exporting a project mixes down automatically
without changing your tracks — see
[Export audio](/manual/manual-index/header/file/export-audio).
