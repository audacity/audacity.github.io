## Selecting clips

Click a clip's **header** to select it, or double-click in its body. A
selected clip shows its handles.

<UIShot
  name="app/SceneClipSelected"
  alt="A selected clip, showing the trim handles at its top corners and the clock speed handles below them"
/>

With a clip selected, holding **Shift** and clicking another clip on the
same track selects both, along with every clip between them. Holding
<Shortcut client:load keys="cmd" /> and clicking picks clips one at a time
instead, on any track. Whatever comes next applies to everything selected.

## Time range selections

A **time range selection** selects audio data directly, without regard to
clip boundaries: click in the waveform and drag left or right. The selected
stretch is highlighted across the track, and dragging downwards as well
selects the same stretch on every track passed over.

<UIShot
  name="app/SceneClipSelection"
  alt="A stretch of a clip selected by dragging, highlighted across the track"
/>

- Holding **Shift** and clicking extends the current selection to the point
  clicked.
- Double-clicking an empty part of a track selects all of that track's
  audio.
- **Select → Select all**, <Shortcut client:load keys="cmd+a" />, selects
  everything in the project.

## Moving clips

Dragging a clip **by its header** moves it: along its track, onto another
track, or into the empty space below the tracks, which creates a new track
to hold it. Dragging inside the body does not move the clip; it makes a
time range selection.

Dropping a clip on top of another is allowed: the clip underneath is
overwritten where they overlap.
[Undo](/manual/menu-bar/edit#undo) restores it.

With [snapping](/manual/toolbar/snapping) on, moved clips land on the
chosen grid.
