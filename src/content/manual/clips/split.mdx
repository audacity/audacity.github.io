**Split** cuts a clip in two at a chosen point. Each half becomes a clip of
its own, so one can be deleted, moved or trimmed without touching the
other.

## The split tool

The split tool lives in the [Tools toolbar](/manual/toolbar/tools#split-tool),
behind the scissors button, and toggles with
<Shortcut client:load keys="s" />. While it is on, a guideline follows the
pointer across the clip, showing where the cut will land.

<UIShot
  name="app/SceneClipSplit"
  state="tool"
  alt="The split tool switched on, with its guideline running through the clip at the pointer"
/>

Clicking applies the split. The portion left of the cut is selected, ready
to delete. Pressing <Shortcut client:load keys="s" /> again switches the
tool back off.

<UIShot
  name="app/SceneClipSplit"
  alt="The clip split in two at the guideline, with the left portion selected"
/>

## Splitting from the keyboard

<Shortcut client:load keys="cmd+i" /> makes the same cut without the tool:
at the edit cursor it splits once, and with a time range selection it cuts
at both edges of the selection, leaving the selected stretch as a clip of
its own.
