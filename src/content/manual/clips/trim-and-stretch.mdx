A selected clip carries two pairs of handles at its edges: the top pair
trim, the lower pair stretch.

## Trim

The top handles **trim**. Hovering one changes the pointer to the trim
cursor; dragging the clip's left or right edge inwards shortens it.

<UIShot
  name="app/SceneClipTrim"
  alt="The pointer on a clip's right trim handle, changed to the trim cursor"
/>

Trimming is non-destructive. The trimmed audio is kept past the clip's
edge, and dragging the edge back out restores it.

[Trim clip](/manual/menu-bar/edit#trim-clip) does the reverse job from a
selection: it keeps the selected stretch and trims away the rest of the
clip.

## Stretch

The lower handles, marked with a clock, change the clip's **speed**.
Hovering one changes the pointer to the stretch cursor; dragging stretches
or squeezes the clip, so it plays slower or faster.

<UIShot
  name="app/SceneClipStretched"
  alt="A stretched clip with its speed badge, the pointer on the clock handle changed to the stretch cursor"
/>

A clip whose speed has been changed shows a badge in its top corner with
the current percentage. Clicking the badge opens a field for typing an
exact value.

## Pitch

Pitch is changed from the clip's menu: right-click the clip, or open its
**⋯** menu, and choose **Open pitch and speed dialog**. It shifts pitch in
semitones and cents, and its **Optimize for voice** option preserves vocal
resonances when shifting speech or singing.
