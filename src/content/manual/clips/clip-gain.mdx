Volume changes inside a clip are drawn as a **gain curve** on the clip
itself, rather than applied as an effect. The curve makes fades, dips and
level rides visible on the waveform, and it stays editable.

<UIShot
  name="app/SceneClipGain-curve"
  alt="A clip gain curve drawn along a clip: the line holds full level, then ramps down to a lower level for the rest of the clip"
/>

The curve is edited with the **clip gain tool**, the
[Clip envelope button](/manual/toolbar/tools#clip-gain) in the Tools
toolbar. While the tool is on:

- Clicking on the line adds a point.
- Dragging a point moves it: down for a volume drop or a fade out, up for a
  fade in.
- The waveform redraws to match the curve, so the level change is visible
  in the audio itself.

Switching the tool off returns clicks to ordinary selection. The curve
remains on the clip and keeps applying.

The curve is drawn on the clip, so it moves with the clip and is saved with
the project.
