Classic is the workspace for readers coming from Audacity 3: the editing
and zoom commands sit in the toolbar as buttons rather than living only in
the menus.

<UIShot
  name="app/MainWindow-classic"
  alt="The Classic workspace: the full toolbar of editing and zoom buttons above the track area"
/>

## What the toolbar shows

- The transport group: Play, Stop, Record, Rewind to start, Rewind to end
  and Toggle loop region.
- The editing tools: Clip gain and the Split tool.
- The clipboard group: Cut, Copy and Paste.
- Trim and Silence.
- The full zoom group: Zoom in, Zoom out, Zoom to selection, Zoom to fit
  project and Zoom toggle.
- The timecode, Snapping, and the record and playback levels.

Classic is the only workspace with the clipboard buttons and the full zoom
group. It does not carry the spectral view toggle.

## Ruler

The timeline ruler is set to Minutes and seconds.
