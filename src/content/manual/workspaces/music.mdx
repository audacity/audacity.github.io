Music is the workspace for material that is written to a tempo. Its
distinguishing feature is that the Timeline ruler is set to **Beats and
Measures** rather than Minutes and Seconds, and the toolbar carries the two
controls that go with it.

## What the toolbar shows

- Play
- Stop
- Record
- Rewind to Start
- Rewind to End
- Toggle Loop Region
- Zoom In
- Zoom Out
- Trim
- Silence
- Timecode
- Tempo
- Time Signature
- Snapping
- Record Level
- Playback Level

Tempo and Time Signature appear here and in no other shipped workspace. The
Split Tool, Toggle Spectral View, the clipboard buttons and the extra zoom
buttons are hidden; all of them remain available from the Header menus.

## Ruler

The Timeline ruler is set to Beats and Measures, so positions and snapping
are expressed in bars and beats.
