A new workspace is always made from the layout you are currently looking at.
Set the window up first, then save it.

  name="app/SceneWorkspaceSelector"

## Setting up the layout

- Use the cog button at the right-hand end of the Transport Toolbar —
  **Customize toolbar** — to show or hide individual toolbar buttons.
- Move the Transport Toolbar, and show or hide panels, until the window
  suits the work you are doing.

## Saving it

Choose **Create new workspace** from the workspace dropdown in the top
right corner of the window, or open **Edit workspaces** and choose
**Save layout as new workspace**. Audacity asks for a **Workspace name**
and adds it to the list.

Names must be unique and cannot be empty; Audacity says so and asks for
another name if you pick one that is already in use.

Your new workspace then appears in the workspace dropdown and in
**View → Workspaces** alongside Classic, Modern and Music, and can be
removed again from Edit workspaces.

## Notes

- A new workspace starts as a copy of the layout in front of you, including
  the built-in workspace you were using at the time.
- Workspaces do not carry global appearance settings such as the theme,
  accent colour or waveform display options.
