A workspace is a named configuration of the interface: which buttons the
[Tools toolbar](/manual/toolbar) carries, how the window is laid out, and,
for Music, which format the [timeline ruler](/manual/timeline) uses.
Switching workspaces changes the layout, not the project.

Workspaces do not carry global appearance settings: the theme, accent
colour and waveform display options stay the same whichever workspace is
active.

## Switching workspace

The **Workspace** dropdown sits in the top right corner of the window; the
same list is in **View → Workspaces**. The first-launch setup also offers a
choice of starting workspace.

## The built-in workspaces

- **[Modern](/manual/workspaces/modern)**: the default. A pared-back
  toolbar for recording, playing and looking around.
- **[Classic](/manual/workspaces/classic)**: every editing button in the
  toolbar, laid out for Audacity 3 habits.
- **[Music](/manual/workspaces/music)**: beats and measures on the ruler,
  with tempo and time signature in the toolbar.

## Your own workspaces

The layout in front of you can be saved as a workspace of your own, and
managed afterwards: see
[Creating new workspaces](/manual/workspaces/creating-new-workspaces) and
[Edit workspaces](/manual/workspaces/edit-workspaces).
