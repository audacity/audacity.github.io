The Tools toolbar holds playback and recording, the editing tools, zoom,
the playhead position, snapping and the record level. It is the only
toolbar that can be moved.

  name="app/ToolbarTools"

/>

## Moving the Tools toolbar

Grab the toolbar's drag handle and drag it:

- Drop it near the top of the window to dock it at the top.
- Drop it near the bottom of the window to dock it at the bottom.
- Drop it anywhere else to leave it floating at that position.

The Project Toolbar and Selection Toolbar stay fixed in place.

## Navigating toolbars with the keyboard

Once a toolbar has focus, arrow keys move between its buttons and controls.

## The controls, by group

- [Transport](/manual/toolbar/transport): play, stop, record, the skips and the loop toggle.
- [Tools](/manual/toolbar/tools): the editing tools, toggles that change what clicking does, and buttons that act on the selection.
- [Zoom tools](/manual/toolbar/zoom-tools): getting closer to the waveform and back out again.
- [Playhead position](/manual/toolbar/playhead-position): the playhead position, as a number you can read and edit.
- [Music](/manual/toolbar/music): tempo and time signature, shown in the Music workspace.
- [Snapping](/manual/toolbar/snapping): snap edits, clips and selections to a grid.
- [Microphone level](/manual/toolbar/microphone-level): the input meter, and the level Audacity records at.
- [Playback meter](/manual/toolbar/playback-meter): the output meter, the playback volume, and the meter's settings.
