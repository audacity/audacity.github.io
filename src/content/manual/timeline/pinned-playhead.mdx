Changes how the view follows the playhead during playback and recording.
With the playhead pinned, it is held in the centre of the view and the audio
scrolls past it. With it unpinned, the playhead moves across a stationary
view, and the view only scrolls when the playhead is about to run off the
edge.

It is off by default, so the playhead moves and the view stays put until it
has to catch up.

## Where to find it

Right-click the timeline ruler and choose **Pinned playhead**. The item is
checked while the playhead is pinned.

## Notes

- This option only matters while
  [Update display while playing](/manual/timeline/update-display-while-playing)
  is on. With that off, the view does not follow the playhead at all.
