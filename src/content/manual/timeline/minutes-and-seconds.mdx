Sets the timeline ruler to clock time — hours, minutes, seconds and, as you
zoom in, fractions of a second. The tick spacing adapts to the zoom level,
running from hours at the widest view down to microseconds at the closest.

This is one of two ruler formats; the other is
[Beats & measures](/manual/timeline/beats-and-measures).
Choosing one turns the other off, and the grid lines behind the tracks follow
whichever is chosen.

## Where to find it

Right-click the timeline ruler and choose **Minutes & seconds**. The item is
checked while it is the active format.
