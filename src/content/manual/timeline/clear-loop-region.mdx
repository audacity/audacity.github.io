Removes the loop region altogether, leaving the upper half of the timeline
ruler empty. This is different from
[Toggle loop region](/manual/timeline/toggle-loop-region),
which only switches looping off and keeps the region for later.

Once the region is cleared, playback runs on to the end of the project as
normal, and you draw a new region from scratch when you need one.

## Where to find it

Right-click the timeline ruler and choose **Clear loop region**, or press

<Shortcut client:load keys="shift+alt+l" />. It is also in the
[Looping](/manual/menu-bar/select#looping) submenu.
