Reverse turns the selected audio back to front, so the last sample becomes the
first. Sounds keep their pitch and content but lose their shape: a note that
decayed now swells, and speech becomes unintelligible.

## Where to find it

**Effect → Special → Reverse**, with audio selected. There is no dialog: the effect
applies as soon as you choose it.

## Notes

The classic use is the reversed swell before a beat — reverse a cymbal crash or
a chord, and it grows into the downbeat instead of decaying away from it.

Reverse is also half of a useful trick with effects that only build forwards.
Reverse the selection, apply a delay or reverb, then reverse it back, and the
tail arrives before the sound instead of after it.

Applying Reverse twice returns the audio exactly to where it started.
