Fades the selection up or down using a curve you shape yourself, rather than
the fixed shape of Fade in and Fade out. Reach for it when you want a gentler
start, a faster tail, or a fade that only drops part of the way rather than
all the way to silence.

<UIShot name="effects/Adjustable_fade" alt="The Adjustable Fade dialog" />

## Where to find it

**Effect → Fading → Adjustable fade**.

## Controls

- **Fade Type** — Fade Up, Fade Down, S-Curve Up or S-Curve Down. Fade Up is
  the default.
- **Mid-fade Adjust (%)** — −100 to 100, default 0. Bends the middle of the
  curve away from a straight line without moving the start and end points.
- **Start/End as** — whether the two level values below are read as
  **% of Original** (the default) or as **dB Gain**.
- **Start (or end)** — the level the fade begins at. Default 0.
- **End (or start)** — the level the fade finishes at. Default 100.
- **Handy Presets (override controls)** — None Selected, Linear In, Linear
  Out, Exponential In, Exponential Out, Logarithmic In, Logarithmic Out,
  Rounded In, Rounded Out, Cosine In, Cosine Out, S-Curve In, S-Curve Out.
  Picking anything other than None Selected overrides the settings above.

## Notes

Which of the two level controls is the start and which is the end depends on
the Fade Type, which is why they are labelled "Start (or end)" and "End (or
start)".

The effect has a preview, so you can hear the shape before committing to it.
