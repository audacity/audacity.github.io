Crossfades between two tracks that overlap in time: one is faded out over the
selection while the other is faded in. Select the same time range in both
tracks and apply the effect once.

<UIShot name="effects/Crossfade_tracks" alt="The Crossfade Tracks dialog" />

## Where to find it

**Effect → Fading → Crossfade tracks**.

## Controls

- **Fade TYPE** — Constant Gain, Constant Power 1, Constant Power 2 or Custom
  Curve. Constant Gain is the default.
- **Custom curve** — 0 to 1, default 0. Sets the curvature used by the Custom
  Curve type; roughly constant power at the 0.5 setting.
- **Fade direction** — Automatic, Alternating Out / In, or Alternating In /
  Out. Automatic is the default.

## Notes

On Automatic, each track is judged on its own: if the selection lies closer
to the start of that track's audio it fades in, and if it lies closer to the
end it fades out. The two alternating settings decide instead from the
track's position in the project, so tracks alternate between fading out and
fading in down the stack.

Constant Gain keeps the summed level of the two tracks steady when the
material is correlated; the Constant Power shapes suit unrelated material,
where an equal-gain crossfade would dip in the middle.
