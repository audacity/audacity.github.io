Change Tempo was an Audacity 3 effect that made a selection faster or slower while holding its pitch. **Audacity 4 does not ship it**, and there is no Change Tempo entry in the Effect menu.

## What to use instead

**[Sliding stretch](/manual/effects/pitch-and-tempo/sliding-stretch)** covers the same ground and more. Set **Initial tempo change** and **Final tempo change** to the same percentage and you get a constant tempo change across the selection, exactly as Change Tempo did; set them to different values and the tempo moves gradually from one to the other.

Pitch is left alone unless you also move the pitch controls, so the substitution is like for like.
