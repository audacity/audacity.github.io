Passes frequencies above the cutoff and progressively attenuates everything
below it. It is the usual first move against rumble, handling noise, air
conditioning and other low-frequency mush on a spoken-word recording.

## Where to find it

**Effect → EQ and filters → High-pass filter**.

## Controls

- **Frequency (Hz)** — the cutoff. Default 1000, minimum 0.
- **Roll-off (dB per octave)** — how steeply the level falls away below the
  cutoff: 6, 12, 24, 36 or 48 dB. Default 6 dB.

## Notes

The cutoff is the point at which attenuation begins, not a hard wall — with a
6 dB roll-off the reduction is gentle and reaches into the passband, while 48
dB cuts away much more sharply just below the cutoff.

For speech, a cutoff somewhere between 60 and 100 Hz removes rumble without
thinning the voice. The effect has a preview, so try a setting before
applying it.
