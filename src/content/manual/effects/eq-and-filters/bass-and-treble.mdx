Bass and Treble is a two-band tone control: one shelf filter lifts or lowers the low end, another does the same at the top. It is the quickest way to warm up or brighten a track when you do not need the precision of a full equaliser.

<UIShot name="effects/Bass_and_Treble" alt="The Bass and Treble dialog" />

## Where to find it

**Effect → EQ and filters → Bass and Treble**, with audio selected.

## Controls

Bass and Treble are knobs on the left, output volume on the right, with a checkbox underneath.

- **Bass** — boost or cut the low end, in dB. Range −30 to 30 dB.
- **Treble** — boost or cut the high end, in dB. Range −30 to 30 dB.
- **Output volume** — a level trim applied after the two filters, in dB. Range −30 to 30 dB.
- **Auto-adjust volume to preserve loudness** — move the output volume automatically as you boost or cut, so the overall level stays roughly where it started.

## Notes

Boosting is the tempting move but cutting is usually the better one: pulling down the band that is in the way leaves more headroom than pushing up the band you want. Watch for clipping if you do boost heavily, and use **Output volume** to pull the result back down.
