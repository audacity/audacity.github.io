Opens a menu for picking the audio hardware Audacity uses. Every list in it
is built from the devices your computer reports, so what you see depends on
what is connected.

<UIShot
  name="app/SceneAudioSetupMenu"
  alt="The Audio setup button with its menu open: Host, Playback device, Recording device, Recording channels, Rescan audio devices and Audio settings"
/>

## Where to find it

**Hotbar → Audio setup**, in the bar across the top of the project window.

## What the menu contains

- **Host** — the audio system Audacity talks to. Which hosts are offered
  depends on your operating system.
- **Playback device** — where audio plays. The list opens with a **System
  default** entry, which names the device it currently resolves to,
  followed by each available output.
- **Recording device** — the same, for inputs.
- **Recording channels** — 1 (Mono) Recording channel or 2 (Stereo)
  Recording channels, up to the number your recording device offers.
- **Rescan audio devices** — rebuild the device lists. Use it after
  plugging in or unplugging an interface.
- **Audio settings** — opens Preferences at the Audio settings page.

## Notes

- The device and host currently in use are ticked.
- The menu rebuilds itself whenever the device list or the audio
  configuration changes, so it stays in step with the hardware.
