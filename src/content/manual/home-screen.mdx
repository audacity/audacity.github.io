The Audacity window is one of two areas at a time, switched by the tabs at
the top left:

- **[Home](/manual/home-screen/home)**: the project browser shown when no
  project is open. It holds [New & recent](/manual/home-screen/home/new-and-recent)
  and, in cloud-enabled builds,
  [Cloud projects and audio files](/manual/home-screen/home/cloud-projects-and-audio-files).
- **[Project](/manual/home-screen/project)**: the editing view, with the
  tracks, toolbars and everything that works on audio.

<UIShot
  name="app/SceneHomeProjects"
  alt="The Home area, with the Home and Project tabs at the top left of the window"
/>

Opening a project from Home switches to Project; the **Home** tab returns
at any time without closing the project.
[Publish](/manual/home-screen/publish) is planned as a third area, and is
not in the current alpha.
