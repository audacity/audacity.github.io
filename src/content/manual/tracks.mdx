Everything you record or import lands in the **track area**: horizontal lanes
stacked down the window, each holding audio as
[clips](/manual/getting-started/basic-audio-editing). A track is the unit the
project is organised by — one voice, one instrument, one imported file — and a
project can hold as many as you need.

<UIShot
  name="app/SceneFirstTake"
  alt="A track in the track area: the control panel on the left, the clip on the right"
/>

Each track has two halves:

- The **[Track Control Panel](/manual/track-control-panel)** on
  the left — its name, volume and pan controls, mute and solo, and the
  Effects button for its realtime stack.
- **[The waveform](/manual/waveform)** on the right — the audio
  itself, drawn so louder moments are taller, alongside the
  **[vertical rulers](/manual/vertical-rulers)** that put a
  scale on it.

New tracks come from **Add track** at the top of the panel, or arrive on
their own when you record or import. For working with what's on them, start
with [basic audio editing](/manual/getting-started/basic-audio-editing).
