The **Export** page holds two behaviours. It appears in cloud-enabled
builds.

- **Export behavior**: whether choosing to export shows the
  **How would you like to export?** dialog first.
- **Save behavior**: what happens on the first save of a project.
  **Always ask** shows the computer-or-cloud choice; **Always save to
  cloud** and **Always save to computer** skip it and go straight to the
  chosen destination.

See [Save your project](/manual/getting-started/save-your-project) for the
save flow these settings control.
