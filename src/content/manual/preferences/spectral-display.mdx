The **Spectral display** page configures the spectrogram view of a track.

## What it covers

- **Algorithm** — the analysis method and window used to build the
  spectrogram.
- **Scale** — the frequency scale and range shown.
- **Colours** — the palette and gain used to map intensity to colour.
- **Selection** — how spectral selections behave.

## Notes

These settings affect how a track is drawn in spectrogram view — see
[Toggle spectral view](/manual/toolbar/tools#toggle-spectral-view).
They do not change the audio.
