The **Appearance** page controls how Audacity looks.

<UIShot name="app/ScenePrefsAppearance" alt="The Appearance preferences page" />

## What it covers

- **Themes** — the overall light or dark appearance.
- **UI colours** — accent and interface colours.
- **UI font** — the typeface and size used across the interface.
- **Clip style** — how clips are drawn in the tracks area.
