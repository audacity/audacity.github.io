The **Plugins** page controls how Audacity finds and loads plugins.

<UIShot name="app/ScenePrefsPlugins" alt="The Plugins preferences page" />

## What it covers

- **Plugin locations** — the folders scanned for VST, LV2 and Audio Unit
  plugins, and rescanning them.
- **Effect options** — how effects are presented and grouped in the menus.
- **Extensions** — settings for installed extensions.

## Notes

Individual plugins are enabled and disabled in the
[Plugin manager](/manual/menu-bar/effect#plugin-manager) rather
than here.
