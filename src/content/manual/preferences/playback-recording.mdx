<UIShot name="app/ScenePrefsPlaybackRecording" alt="The Playback/Recording preferences page" />

## What it covers

- **Cursor** — how the playhead behaves during and after playback.
- **Playback performance** — settings affecting smooth playback.
- **Recording behaviour** — options applied when a recording starts.
- **Lead-in recording** — the amount of existing audio played before
  recording begins, so you can catch the timing of a punch-in.
