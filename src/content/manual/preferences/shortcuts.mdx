The **Shortcuts** page lists every command that can carry a keyboard
shortcut and lets you reassign them, restore a default, or resolve a
conflict where two commands share a binding.

## Notes

The shipped defaults are listed in full on the
[keyboard shortcuts reference](/manual/shortcuts) page.
