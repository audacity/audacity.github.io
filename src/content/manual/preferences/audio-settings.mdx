The **Audio settings** page configures how Audacity talks to your audio
hardware.

<UIShot name="app/ScenePrefsAudioSettings" alt="The Audio settings preferences page" />

## What it covers

- **Audio API** — the driver system used to reach your devices, and on
  Windows the ASIO options where available.
- **Sample rate** — the project sample rate used for new projects.
- **Buffer and latency** — the buffer size, and the latency compensation
  applied when recording alongside existing tracks.
- **Meter dB range** — the range the level meters display.

## Notes

Buffer size trades stability against latency: larger buffers are safer on
slower machines, smaller buffers respond faster when overdubbing.
