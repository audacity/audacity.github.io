Docked along the bottom of the window, the Bottom toolbar shows the current
selection as three figures: **Selection** start and end, and **Duration**.

<UIShot
  name="app/ToolbarBottom"
  alt="The Bottom toolbar: selection start, end and duration readouts"
/>

All three are editable — click a digit and type. A new start or end moves
that edge of the selection; a new duration moves the end so the selection
becomes exactly that long. The readouts are greyed out when nothing is
selected.

<UIShot
  name="readouts/TimecodeStartEnd"
  alt="The selection start and end readouts, close up"
/>

## Formats

The start-and-end pair and the duration each carry their own format — clock
time, samples, frames, `beats:bars` and more — chosen the same way as the
[playhead timecode](/manual/toolbar/selection#timecode). You can read the
selection in clock time while reading its length in seconds, or the other
way round. In `beats:bars`, the figures follow the project
[tempo](/manual/toolbar/music#tempo) and
[time signature](/manual/toolbar/music#time-signature).
