Every track in a project has a control panel down the left-hand side of the
Tracks area. The panel is where the track is named, where its playback
controls live, and where its menu is opened from. Above the panels sits a
**Tracks** title bar with the **Add track** button.

  name="app/SceneSpotlightWaveTrackItem"

/>

All panels share the same three elements: an icon showing the track type, an
editable track name, and a menu button. What appears below that depends on the
track type — an audio track adds pan, volume, mute, solo, an Effects button
and level meters, while a label track adds a single **Add label** button.

## Working with the panel

- Double-click the track name to rename it in place.
- Drag the bottom edge of a panel to change the track's height. Reduce it far
  enough and the track collapses: mute and solo move up beside the name and
  the remaining controls are hidden.
- Drag a panel up or down to reorder the tracks.
- Right-click anywhere on the panel, or use the menu button beside the name,
  to open the track's menu.

## Add track

**Add track** sits at the right of the **Tracks** title bar, above the track
control panels. Clicking it opens a small popup offering three track types:

- **Mono** — a single-channel audio track.
- **Stereo** — a two-channel audio track.
- **Label** — a track that holds labels rather than audio.

Choosing one creates the track immediately and closes the popup. Clicking
**Add track** again while the popup is open closes it.

The same three track types can be created from the Tracks menu:
[New mono track](/manual/menu-bar/tracks#new-mono-track) — which
also has the default shortcut <Shortcut client:load keys="cmd+shift+n" /> —
[New stereo track](/manual/menu-bar/tracks#new-stereo-track) and
[New label track](/manual/menu-bar/tracks#new-label-track).
