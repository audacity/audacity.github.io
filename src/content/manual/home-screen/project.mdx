**Project** is the projects browser, and the item the Home screen opens on:
every project you can open, in one list.

<UIShot
  name="app/SceneHomeProjects"
  alt="The projects browser: the search field, the tabs, and the New & recent grid"
/>

## What is on the screen

- A **search** field that filters the list as you type.
- Tabs for [New & recent](/manual/home-screen/project/new-and-recent)
  and, when cloud support is enabled,
  [Cloud projects and Cloud audio files](/manual/home-screen/project/cloud-projects-and-audio-files).
- A **Grid view** / **List view** pair of buttons, and, on the cloud tabs,
  a **Refresh** button.
- **New** and **Open other…** buttons along the bottom.

The tab and the view type you last used are remembered.
