**Cloud account** shows the audio.com account Audacity is connected to. The
item appears in the Home screen's menu only when cloud support is enabled.

<UIShot
  name="app/SceneHomeProjects-account"
  alt="The Cloud account view before signing in: an Audio.com card with Sign in and Create account buttons"
/>

Before signing in, the card offers **Sign in** and **Create account**.
Signing in connects Audacity to audio.com, which is what
[saving to the cloud](/manual/getting-started/save-your-project#save-to-the-cloud)
and the
[Cloud projects and audio files](/manual/home-screen/project/cloud-projects-and-audio-files)
tabs use. Signing out empties those tabs again.
