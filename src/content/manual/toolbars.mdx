Audacity 4 has three toolbars, and each has its own page.

## Project toolbar

<UIShot
  name="app/ToolbarProject"
  alt="The Project toolbar: Home and Project tabs, Audio setup, Share audio and Get effects, the workspace selector, and undo and redo"
/>

Across the very top of the window: the **Home** and **Project** tabs, the
hotbar (Audio setup, Share audio, Get effects), the workspace selector, and
undo and redo. [Project toolbar →](/manual/project-toolbar)

## Tools toolbar

<UIShot
  name="app/ToolbarTools"
  alt="The Tools toolbar: transport controls, editing tools, zoom, the playhead timecode, snapping, and the record level button"
/>

The one you work in: playback and recording, the editing tools, zoom, the
playhead position, snapping, and the record level. It is also the one toolbar
you can move. [Tools toolbar →](/manual/toolbar)

## Bottom toolbar

<UIShot
  name="app/ToolbarBottom"
  alt="The Bottom toolbar: selection start and end, and duration"
/>

Docked along the bottom: the current selection's start, end and duration,
each editable. [Bottom toolbar →](/manual/bottom-toolbar)

## Workspaces change what you see

The **workspace** selector in the Project toolbar switches between
[Classic, Modern, Music and Spectral editing](/manual/workspaces)
layouts, and the choice changes which tools the Tools toolbar shows — Music
adds tempo and time-signature controls, Spectral editing pares the toolbar
down to zoom and a view switch.
